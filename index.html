About Us

More Than a School, It’s a Community

Brew Masters Coffee Academy is a coffee education and consulting business built around three ideas: train people in the craft of coffee, consult for coffee businesses, and connect everyone who loves coffee.

Whether you are pouring your first espresso or shaping a café menu, you learn by doing, with practical skills you can use the next day.

Get in Touch
